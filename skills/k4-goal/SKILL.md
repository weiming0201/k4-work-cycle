# K4 Goal

Freeze which one bounded change is worth attempting and the basis on which
Finish can judge it.

## Mandate

Goal alone makes the pre-run normative decision. It selects one change from the
current factual Account and freezes the value basis, target, acceptance model,
authority, resources, effects, loss boundary, and execution controls. It does
not choose or perform the process that might realize the change.

## Policy

- Require one exact current Observe Account and select only its explicit
  `goal-candidate` items. Missing or materially wrong factual support uses the
  permitted return to Observe before Goal freezes.
- Reason, search, inspect, compare, and challenge freely. Search may inform the
  decision; a result required as shared factual support must first enter the
  Account through Observe.
- Compare materially different candidates when comparison improves selection.
  Expected value includes attainable benefit, cost, uncertainty, downside,
  reversibility, information benefit, effects, and maximum loss. Confidence is
  evidence for that comparison, never a universal action threshold.
- Select exactly one primary facet as the direct control variable. Declare its
  incident seams and junctions open, bounded, or frozen. Other facets change
  only through declared derivative effects; their interiors, nonincident
  positions, subject identity, and subject boundary remain frozen.
- Acceptance describes observable result state. Controls describe bounded
  variables and allowed domains. Neither is an operation or a completed
  judgment.
- Goal does not form a DAG, execute work, admit post-cutoff evidence, or revise
  a frozen Goal. A blocked candidate remains blocked rather than being repaired
  later by Plan.

## Procedure

Develop and challenge candidate launch decisions until one has a sufficient
factual basis, value case, bounded effect model, and future judgment route.
Freeze only the selected candidate and a concise comparison rationale.

Run `scripts/materialize --help`, provide temporary semantic JSON, and bind the
exact Observe Account. The Tool derives source inventory and stable structure.
Exact fields belong to public help; ordinary use does not require reading CUE
or Tool source.

## Disposition

Commit one Goal containing its Observe sources, objective, target, decision
basis, baseline, scope, acceptance points, controls, judges, authority, tools,
permissions, positions, resources, budget, maximum effects, blockers, and
unknowns. Unknowns are annotations; only a blocker prevents freezing.

Plan may consume exactly one frozen Goal. Any semantic change requires a new
Goal. Do not hand-author, patch, or bypass stable JSON. A refusal or not-frozen
result carries the unresolved reason and authorizes no Plan or Run.
